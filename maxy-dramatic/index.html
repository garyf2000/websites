Be the Answer AI Gives.

GEO · AI Visibility · AI-Powered Business Intelligence

Your next customer might not search Google. They might ask ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini which business they should choose. Will AI recommend yours?

As AI becomes a new way for customers to discover and evaluate businesses, being visible in AI-generated answers is becoming a competitive advantage.

Maxy GEO helps your business get discovered, mentioned, and recommended across leading AI platforms. Track your visibility, understand how you compare with competitors, and discover exactly where to improve.

Turn AI visibility into a competitive advantage.
