About Us

Built to last, done right the first time

At Evergreen Ridge Landscaping, we believe every outdoor project should be built with care, precision, and lasting quality. From driveways and patios to walkways and entryways, our team focuses on creating spaces that look great and perform reliably for years to come.

We know that proper installation goes far beyond appearances. Careful grading, solid preparation, effective drainage, and precise material placement all play an important role in preventing issues such as shifting pavers, uneven surfaces, and premature wear.

Our goal is simple: deliver dependable workmanship without cutting corners. We take pride in paying attention to the details and completing every project to a standard we can stand behind.
